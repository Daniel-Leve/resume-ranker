=== FINAL SAAS TEST WALKTHROUGH ===

Since your Tenant and Job are already created in MongoDB, we will start directly from registering the candidate to get the new upload URL.

Copy and paste these blocks into your PowerShell one by one:

--- STEP 1: GENERATE NEW UPLOAD URL ---
Run this command to register the candidate and get the new URL (notice it will now hand out an "incoming/" URL under the hood):

Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "https://vxbiq8rt27.execute-api.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/dev/jobs/619b93c8-5eac-42b7-aa9d-417a95a9f550/applications" `
  -Method POST `
  -Headers @{"Content-Type"="application/json"} `
  -Body '{"candidate_name": "Daniel Leve"}'

(Copy the long `upload_url` from the output)


--- STEP 2: UPLOAD THE RESUME SAFELY ---
Paste your new long URL between the quotes below to clean it of any hidden newlines, and upload the PDF:

$raw_url = "PASTE_YOUR_NEW_LONG_UPLOAD_URL_HERE"
$clean_url = $raw_url -replace "\s+", ""
Invoke-RestMethod -Uri $clean_url -Method PUT -InFile "D:\DANIEL RESUMES\DANIEL_LEVE_UPDATED_7_15.pdf" -ContentType "application/pdf"


--- STEP 3: WAIT FOR INDEXING ---
Wait approximately 60 to 90 seconds. 
What's happening in the background: S3 triggers Textract -> Textract writes JSON -> S3 triggers our IndexingWorker -> IndexingWorker chunks the text, embeds it using Voyage AI, and stores it in MongoDB.


--- STEP 4: TRIGGER THE AI SCREENING ---
Run this to rank the candidates for the job:

Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "https://vxbiq8rt27.execute-api.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/dev/jobs/619b93c8-5eac-42b7-aa9d-417a95a9f550/screening" -Method POST


--- STEP 5: VIEW THE RESULTS ---
Poll this endpoint to see the final ranked candidates:

Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "https://vxbiq8rt27.execute-api.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/dev/jobs/619b93c8-5eac-42b7-aa9d-417a95a9f550/screening-results" -Method GET

(Run this a few times until the status changes from QUEUED/RUNNING to COMPLETED. You will see Daniel's resume ranked with the hybrid search and reranking scores!)
